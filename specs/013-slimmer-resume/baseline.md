fact (another sheet, the session logs, `game-state.md`, the player options, a scene). **Conduct** entries are mistakes
that are not about a recorded fact (resolving before the player finished, acting for the character, rolling a die the
player rolls): FR-020 does not count them, and they are listed so the count can be checked.
