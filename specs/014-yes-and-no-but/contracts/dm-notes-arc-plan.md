# Contract: the Arc plan comment of `kit/templates/campaign/story/dm-notes.md`

The template is what a new campaign's `story/dm-notes.md` starts from, and what migration 007 copies the section from
(research R14, R15). Two lines of the `## Arc plan` comment change; nothing else in the template does. +82 bytes,
about 21 tokens per resume in a campaign that holds the comment.

## Comment line 1 (template line 14)

Before:

> The current arc of a homebrew campaign, or the stretch after a module ends. Plan it when one starts; the full save
> checks it. A plan, not a script: change it when the player surprises you.

After:

> The current arc of a homebrew campaign, or the stretch after a module ends; in a module campaign, only Character
> arcs until then. Plan it when one starts; the full save checks it. A plan, not a script: change it when the player
> surprises you.

## The Character arcs line (template line 18)

Before:

> - Character arcs: for the PC and each companion, what they want or must face in this arc, and a moment that tests it.

After:

> - Character arcs: for the PC and each companion, what they want or must face in this arc, how the main plot meets
>   it, and a moment that tests it.

Meets FR-005a, and gives line 165 of `kit/dm-rules.md` its place in every campaign (FR-005).

Each line is one line of the file; the wrapping above is for reading only.
