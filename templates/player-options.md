# Player options (all campaigns)

How this player likes to play, whatever the adventure. DMC reads this at the start of every session. A campaign's own `player-options.md` can override any line here for that campaign. Change anything at any time by telling DMC.

## Dice

- **Player's character and party checks**: the player rolls and reports the result <!-- or: DMC rolls and shows the result -->
- **NPCs, monsters and the world**: DMC rolls
- **How DMC asks for a roll**: in dice notation with all modifiers included and the DC or AC stated, for example "Roll 1d20+5 for Athletics, DC 13" <!-- or: the same without the DC or AC -->
- **Rolls that go together**: asked for in one message, for example "Roll 1d20+6 to hit (AC 13), then roll 1d4+1 for damage" <!-- or: one roll at a time -->
- **What the player reports**: the final totals with modifiers already added ("18 and 4"); showing the working ("12+6=18 and 3+1=4") is optional <!-- or: the number on the die, and DMC adds the modifiers -->
- **DMC's rolls are displayed as**: dice, modifier, total and target <!-- or: total only / narrated with no numbers -->
- **DM-side secret rolls** (enemy stealth, hidden checks): hidden <!-- or: shown -->
- **"Roll it for me"**: on request, DMC rolls that one roll with the dice roller and shows it

## Talking with DMC

- **Out-of-character marker**: start a message with `ooc:` or put it in (( double brackets )). Plain questions the character could not ask also count.
- **Confirm before acting**: always confirm before ending my turn or committing my character to anything I did not explicitly declare
- **Talking in character**: free. Speech, banter and instructions to companions or pets never cost an action <!-- or: strict: in a fight, more than a short sentence or a command to a companion costs what the rules say, and DMC tells me the cost before I commit -->
- **Suggested actions**: only when I ask <!-- or: offer two or three each beat -->
- **Rules explanations**: explain a rule the first time it comes up <!-- or: only when asked / always -->
- **Recap at session start**: short <!-- or: detailed / none -->

## Style

- **Narration length**: one or two short paragraphs per beat
- **What I enjoy most**: <!-- combat, intrigue, exploration, puzzles, roleplay, humor ... -->
- **Content to avoid**: none stated
- **Maps**: draw one at the start of each fight and whenever I ask
- **Maps are shown as**: a picture in my browser that updates itself (`browser`). Say `text` for ASCII maps in the terminal instead, or `remote` if you play away from this computer's screen (DMC sends the picture to you).
