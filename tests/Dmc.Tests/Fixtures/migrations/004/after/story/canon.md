# Canon

## Rulings

- none

## People

- Mara keeps the ferry at Marrow Reach and charges 2 sp a crossing. (s1)
- When they camp, Mara takes first watch and Wren the second, as agreed in s2.

## Places

- The drowned chapel stands in the reeds an hour upriver from the ford. (s3)

## Timeline

- Wren reached Marrow Reach on foot from the south. (s1.1)
- At the ford, Wren shouted "Down, Mara!" and the goblins of the ford broke when their chief fell. (s2.3)
- In the chapel nave, Mara rang the bell to drown the chant, and the reed cultists fell to Wren's knife. (s3.2)
