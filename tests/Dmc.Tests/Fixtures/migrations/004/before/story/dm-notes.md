## The central conflict

The Drowned Saint's cult is raising the old dam to flood Marrow Reach and take the river for itself.

## Arc plan

- Stage 1, exposition: done. Wren learned the cult exists and that Mara hides the barge's cargo.
- Stage 2, rising action: the cult's agents close in on the ferry.
  - Beat: the goblins ambush the ford crossing.
  - Beat: Wren finds the drowned ledger in the chapel.
  - Beat: Vex offers a deal for the ledger.
- Stage 3, climax: the dam.
- Milestone: level 4 when the dam is stopped.
