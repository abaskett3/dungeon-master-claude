# Mara Quill

- **Role in the story**: Wren's sworn companion, a scout of the Fen Watch
- **Species, occupation, age**: half-elf, scout, 24
- **Usually found**: with the party
- **First appeared**: session 1
- **Status**: alive

## Playing them

- **Appearance**: lean, freckled, a green hood patched at the elbow, a longbow wrapped in oilcloth
- **Voice** (speech pattern, mannerism, a sample line): clipped and dry, whistles before bad news: "Well. That's a problem with teeth."
- **Names they use** (for people, places and things that matter, where they differ from DMC's labels; what they would never call them): calls Wren "boss" and the Fen Watch "the Watch"; never says the Drowned King's name
- **Personality**: patient, sly, loyal to a fault
- **What they want**: to clear her brother's name
- **What they fear**: deep water
- **Attitude to the PC, and why**: fiercely loyal since Wren pulled her from the sinkhole (s1.3)

## What they know

- The fen paths between the ford and the weir, by night too.

## Secrets

- Her brother is alive and serves the Drowned King.

## Stat block

- **Based on**: scout
- **AC**: 14 (leather, Dex)
- **Max HP**: 22
- **Speed**: 30 ft
- **STR / DEX / CON / INT / WIS / CHA**: 11 / 16 / 12 / 11 / 13 / 11
- **Saves and skills**: Perception +5, Stealth +6, Survival +5
- **Senses and languages**: darkvision 60 ft, passive Perception 15; Common, Elvish
- **Traits**: Keen Hearing and Sight (advantage on Perception checks by sight or hearing)
- **Actions** (each attack: to hit, damage, reach or range, anything it needs): Multiattack (two attacks); longbow +6, 1d8+3 piercing, range 150/600 ft; shortsword +6, 1d6+3 piercing, reach 5 ft
- **Spells or special abilities**: none
- **Gear**: longbow and 30 arrows, shortsword, leather armour, a fen map, a tin whistle

## History

- s1.3: pulled from the sinkhole by Wren; swore to follow her.
- s3.1: found the ford path in the fog.
