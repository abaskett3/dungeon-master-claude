# Canon

Facts established in play. Once something is here, it is true and stays true.

- Grappling a creature on the ice gives the grappler disadvantage on the check. (s1.3)

## People

- Marta Greel runs the ferry across Grey Tarn. (s1.2)

- Tarnhold is a walled village on the north shore of Grey Tarn. (s1.1)
- The Ember Wood lies a day's walk south of the tarn. (s2.1)

## Factions and powers

- The Lantern Wardens keep the beacons lit along the tarn road. (s1.1)

## History and lore

- The tarn froze solid for the first time in living memory forty winters ago. (s1.1)

## Items of note
