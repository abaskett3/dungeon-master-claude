# People of grey-tarn

## Rook

- **Player character**
- **Species and background**: human, soldier
- **Class and level**: Fighter 3
- **Max HP**: 24
- **Armor Class** (and what it comes from): 16 (chain shirt, shield)
- **Equipped**: silvered spear, shield
- **Appearance**: broad, scarred chin, grey cloak
- **Backstory**: deserted from the southern levy after the siege of Corrow
- s1: bought a silvered spear from Brannoc on credit.
- s2: killed the Ember Witch; took the Ember Key.

## Sela Frostborn

- **Companion**
- **Species and background**: frost genasi, guild artisan
- **Class and level**: Ranger 3
- **Max HP**: 19
- **Resistances, immunities, vulnerabilities**: none since s2.3 (the species gives immunity to cold; the witch's curse burned it out)
- **Appearance**: pale blue skin, white braid, a burn scar on her left hand
- **Names they use**: calls Brannoc "old man", never by name
- **Backstory**: apprenticed to Brannoc in Tarnhold until she was sixteen
- Sela lost her immunity to cold when the Ember Witch's curse burned it out of her. (s2.3)
- s2: the Ember Witch's dying curse took her immunity to cold.
- The witch's dying curse burned the cold out of Sela: she shivers now like anyone else. (s2.3)

## Brannoc Ashby

- Brannoc Ashby is the smith of Tarnhold and Sela's old teacher. (s1.1)
- **Role in the story**: Tarnhold's smith; Sela's old teacher
- **Species, occupation, age**: human, smith, 58
- **Usually found**: the smithy by Tarnhold's east gate
- **First appeared**: session 1
- **Status**: alive
- **Appearance**: bald, soot in his beard, forearms like hams
- **Voice** (speech pattern, mannerism, a sample line): slow, taps the anvil before he answers; "Iron listens. People don't."
- **Names they use**: calls the tarn "the Grey", and Sela "girl"
- **Personality**: gruff, fair, proud of his work
- **What they want**: his 40 gp, and Sela to come home
- **What they fear**: that the key he forged will be found out
- **Attitude to the PC, and why**: wary of Rook, a deserter who owes him money
- He knows Sela lost her cold immunity; she has not told him, but he saw her shiver.
- Sela Frostborn: his former apprentice.
- **Based on**: Veteran
- **AC**: 17
- **Max HP**: 58
- **Actions** (each attack: to hit, damage, reach or range, anything it needs): warhammer +5, 1d8+3 bludgeoning
- s1: sold Rook the silvered spear for 40 gp, on credit.
