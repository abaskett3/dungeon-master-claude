# Places of grey-tarn

## Tarnhold

- Tarnhold is a walled village on the north shore of Grey Tarn. (s1.1)

## Grey Tarn

- The tarn froze solid for the first time in living memory forty winters ago. (s1.1)
- The ferry stops running after the first deep snow, Old Hesketh says. (s2.1)

## The Ember Wood

- The Ember Wood lies a day's walk south of the tarn. (s2.1)
- The witch's hut in the Ember Wood burned down after the witch died. (s2.3)
